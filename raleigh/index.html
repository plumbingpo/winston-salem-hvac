How Raleigh Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement.

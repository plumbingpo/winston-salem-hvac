The Two Repairs We Get Called For Most

Two systems do most of the work in a typical home, and they're the two we repair most. Here's what to expect when you call.

AC Repair in Winston-Salem

Most air conditioner breakdowns trace back to one failed part: a weak capacitor, a burned contactor, a seized fan motor, or a slow refrigerant leak. We diagnose it with gauges and meters, not guesswork. Every repair comes with an upfront quote and an honest opinion on repair versus replacement.

  • Warm air coming from the vents
  • Outdoor unit humming but not starting
  • Frozen coil or iced-over lines
  • Water leaking near the indoor unit

Furnace Repair in Winston-Salem

A furnace that won't light on a cold night can't wait until morning. Most no-heat calls come down to a dirty flame sensor, a failed igniter, or a blower motor, and our trucks carry the common parts to fix them on the spot. We also check for the problems that matter for safety, like a cracked heat exchanger, and give you an upfront price before any work begins.

  • Furnace clicks but never lights
  • Short cycling on and off
  • Cold air from the vents in heating mode
  • New noises when the furnace kicks on

Common Problems

Recognize These HVAC Warning Signs?

HVAC trouble rarely announces itself politely. If you're seeing any of these problems, call us today for expert help.

AC Blowing Warm Air

Warm air from the vents usually traces to a weak capacitor, a refrigerant leak, or a frozen coil, all fixable, and all diagnosable in one visit.

AC repair

Furnace Won't Stay On

Frequent on-off cycling is the furnace protecting itself from a real problem. Ignoring it risks the heat exchanger, the most expensive part in the cabinet.

Furnace repair

Rising Bills, Weak Airflow

Energy bills creeping up while comfort drops means the system is working harder than it should, dirty coils, clogged filters, or failing motors are the usual suspects.

Maintenance & tune-ups

Old System, Repeat Repairs

R-22 refrigerant, rust on the cabinet, a compressor on its second capacitor, these are signs the smart money is on replacement.

System replacement

Dusty Air & Musty Smells

Your ducts move the same air thousands of times a day. Years of dust, dander, and construction debris ride along until they're cleaned out.

Air duct cleaning

Rooms That Never Get Comfortable

Additions and bonus rooms rarely get enough duct capacity. A dedicated ductless head fixes the problem permanently.

Two situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Keeping Your Asheville HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of the year.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.

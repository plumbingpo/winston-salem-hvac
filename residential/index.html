Honest, Upfront Pricing

No homeowner likes surprises on the final bill. Most HVAC failures send small signals well before the system stops. Watch for rising utility bills, airflow that feels weaker than it used to, frost on the copper lines, or rooms that never catch up to the thermostat. Behind those symptoms are usually pinhole refrigerant leaks, worn blower bearings, or a capacitor on its way out. A quick inspection at the first sign usually costs far less than a failed compressor.

Not every call is an emergency. Many Winston-Salem homeowners ask us to size up an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. Maintenance Plans

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Each maintenance visit from Winston-Salem HVAC is far more than a quick filter swap. The technician cleans the coils, verifies the refrigerant charge, tests capacitors and contactors, inspects the burners and heat exchanger, clears the condensate drain, replaces the filter, and checks static pressure in the duct system. Small problems are caught while they're still cheap to fix, and the equipment stays running at full efficiency.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's comfort needs, and your local climate.

Think of maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most families, the plan costs far less than an early replacement.

Proper Sizing

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

When it comes to HVAC, bigger is not better. An oversized unit short-cycles, wastes energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs nonstop and still falls behind on the hottest days. That's why we never just copy the tonnage of the old unit. Instead, we run a proper load calculation using your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

Choosing new equipment is about more than a SEER2 number. Our technician will compare heat pumps against a furnace-and-AC pair, and ducted against ductless, laying out operating costs, electrical and venting work, and the installation itself. Growing families frequently do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds tighter temperatures across the whole house and uses less energy. You choose what fits your home and budget.

Frequent Failures

Heating and Cooling Problems We Solve in Winston-Salem

Homes in Winston-Salem run into a familiar set of heating and cooling failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Winston-Salem HVAC handles them all: no cooling, air that blows but never gets cold, furnaces that won't ignite or short-cycle, frozen evaporator coils, refrigerant leaks, condensers that hum without starting, thermostats that lose their settings, clogged condensate drains, and mini-splits flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

The correct fix depends entirely on what actually failed. On cooling systems, it's usually a capacitor, contactor, or fan motor. On furnaces, it's more often an igniter, flame sensor, or control board. Leaky ductwork bleeding conditioned air into the attic is sealed and repaired, and refrigerant is recovered and recharged once the leak itself is repaired. A few of these problems are simply a nuisance, but a cracked heat exchanger or a clogged condensate drain can be a genuine safety or water-damage risk, and we treat it accordingly.
